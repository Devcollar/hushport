What’s new in beta 2 v0.1.0-beta.2

  • Idle power mode — Mac stops sending silent packets after 30 seconds while streaming stays enabled; audio resumes automatically.
  • Reliable auto-discovery — iPhone found via Bonjour on your Wi‑Fi; manual IP rarely needed.
  • Stable control channel — faster ping/pong verification and fewer reconnect issues.
  • iPhone stays listening during Mac silence — no false “reconnecting” churn.
