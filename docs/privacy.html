Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 31, 2026

HushPort is made by Devcollar Private Limited. This policy describes how the HushPort Mac and iPhone apps handle information during the free public beta.

Summary

  • No user accounts — you do not sign up or log in.
  • No cloud audio — audio streams directly between your Mac and iPhone on your local network.
  • No advertising or analytics SDKs in the current beta builds.
  • Pairing data stays on your devices — trusted device names and network addresses are stored locally.

What HushPort accesses

  • Local network — to discover your other device and send audio/control messages on your Wi‑Fi.
  • Camera (iPhone) — only when you scan a pairing QR code. Video is not recorded or uploaded.
  • Microphone / audio — Mac captures system audio routed to the HushPort virtual output; iPhone plays received audio locally.

What we do not collect

The beta apps do not send your audio, pairing codes, device contents, or personal information to Devcollar servers. There is no central HushPort backend in this version.

GitHub

If you choose to report a bug via GitHub Issues, your report is governed by GitHub’s privacy policy. Please avoid posting sensitive information in public issues.

Source code

HushPort source is publicly visible on GitHub for transparency. See our license for use restrictions.

Children

HushPort is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy as the product moves toward App Store / TestFlight distribution. The date above will change when we do.

Contact

Questions: open a GitHub issue or contact Devcollar through your usual support channel.
